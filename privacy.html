Privacy Policy — Miles

Effective date: October 30, 2026

Miles ("the app") is a mileage tracker developed by Maksim Leontev (CozyHome). This policy explains what data the app uses and how. In short: your trips stay on your iPhone. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your data.

Data the app uses

  • Location (precise, including in the background). Used only to detect drives, measure trip distance and record the route of each trip. If you allow "Always" access, the app can detect and record trips while closed.
  • Motion & Fitness activity. Used only on the device to recognize when you are driving so trips can start and stop automatically.
  • Trip data you create — dates, times, distance, route, start/end addresses, category, notes and settings (such as your deduction rate).

Where data is stored

All trip data and settings are stored locally on your device in the app's private storage, protected by iOS data protection. The app has no account system, no servers, no cloud sync, no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs. Your data may be included in your own iCloud/iTunes device backup, as controlled by your iOS settings.

Apple services

To show street addresses for the start and end of a trip, the app sends those coordinates to Apple's geocoding service (Core Location), and trip maps are displayed with Apple Maps (MapKit). These requests are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy and are not linked to you by us.

Sharing and export

Data leaves your device only when you choose to export trips to a CSV file and save or share it yourself. Where it goes then is your choice.

Your choices

  • You can change or revoke Location and Motion permissions anytime in iOS Settings.
  • You can delete any trip in the app; deleting the app deletes all its data from the device.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
